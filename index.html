About

Who I am

I'm an Applied Math major at UMD working toward a second major in Computer Science. I want to work on quantum computing and machine learning, mainly the algorithm and software side.

In high school I ran a simulated stock fund for DECA. I built my own system for picking stocks, and the fund returned 38.18% while the S&P 500 returned 3.41%. That made me Pennsylvania's only Stock Market Game qualifier at the international conference, where I had to pitch the fund to judges and defend every pick.

Since 2022 I've tutored a student in Ukraine in English through ENGin. When her mom thought our anime-based lessons were a distraction, I ran a test: two topics, two methods. The traditional lesson got a 73%. The anime lesson got a 100%. I kept teaching my way.

At UMD I'm in the Undergraduate Quantum Association and the Maryland Debate Society. I'm also treasurer of a newly formed poker club.
